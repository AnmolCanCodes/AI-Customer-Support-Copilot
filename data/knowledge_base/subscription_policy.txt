# Subscription Policy

## Subscription Plans

The company offers monthly and annual subscription plans.

Customers can select the plan that best fits their needs during signup.

The features and pricing associated with each plan are displayed before the customer completes the purchase.

## Starting a Subscription

A subscription becomes active after a successful payment.

Customers can access the features included in their selected plan while the subscription remains active.

## Subscription Renewal

Subscriptions automatically renew at the end of each billing period unless the customer cancels the subscription before the next renewal date.

Monthly subscriptions renew every month.

Annual subscriptions renew every 12 months.

Customers are responsible for canceling their subscription before the next billing date if they do not want it to renew.

## Canceling a Subscription

Customers can cancel their subscription from their account subscription settings.

After cancellation, the customer normally retains access to the subscription features until the end of the already-paid billing period.

Canceling a subscription prevents future automatic renewals.

Cancellation does not automatically issue a refund for the current billing period.

Customers must separately request a refund if they believe they are eligible under the refund policy.

## Changing Subscription Plans

Customers may be able to upgrade or downgrade their subscription depending on the available plans.

Plan changes may affect the customer's billing amount.

Customers should review the displayed price and billing information before confirming a plan change.

## Upgrading a Subscription

When a customer upgrades their plan, access to the additional features may become available immediately after the plan change is successfully processed.

Any additional charge is calculated according to the applicable billing rules.

## Downgrading a Subscription

When a customer downgrades their plan, the change may take effect at the next billing cycle.

The customer should review the plan-change information displayed during the downgrade process.

## Expired Subscriptions

If a subscription expires or becomes inactive because of a failed payment, access to subscription features may be restricted.

Customers can restore access by resolving the payment issue and maintaining an active subscription.

## Reactivating a Subscription

Customers whose subscription has been canceled may be able to start a new subscription from their account.

The new subscription will be subject to the pricing and terms available at the time of reactivation.

## Subscription Support

For subscription-related issues that cannot be resolved through account settings, customers should contact support.

Support may request the customer's account email and subscription details to investigate the issue.
