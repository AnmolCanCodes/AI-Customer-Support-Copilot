# Refund Policy

## Refund Eligibility

Customers can request a refund within 14 days of their original purchase.

Refund requests submitted after 14 days are normally not eligible for a refund unless an exception is approved by a support manager.

The 14-day refund period begins on the date of the original purchase.

## Subscription Renewal Refunds

A customer may request a refund for a subscription renewal if the request is submitted within 14 days of the renewal charge.

Refund eligibility is reviewed based on the date of the specific charge being disputed.

## How to Request a Refund

Customers who want to request a refund should contact customer support.

The customer should provide:

* Account email address
* Date of purchase or renewal
* Reason for the refund request

Providing accurate information helps the support team process the request more quickly.

## Refund Review

Refund requests are reviewed by the support team.

The support team checks the purchase date, subscription status, payment information, and reason for the request.

If the request meets the refund policy requirements, the refund will be approved.

Requests that fall outside the standard policy may require approval from a support manager.

## Refund Processing Time

After a refund is approved, the company initiates the refund to the original payment method.

The time required for the refund to appear in the customer's account depends on the payment provider or bank.

Customers should allow several business days for the refund to appear.

## Non-Refundable Situations

Purchases outside the 14-day refund period are normally not refundable.

However, exceptional circumstances may be reviewed by a support manager.

## Canceling a Subscription

Canceling a subscription does not automatically result in a refund.

Customers who want both cancellation and a refund must separately request a refund.

Refund eligibility is determined according to the refund policy.

## Refund Status

Customers who have already received confirmation that a refund was approved can contact support if the refund has not appeared after the expected processing period.

Support may request the refund confirmation date and transaction information when investigating a delayed refund.
