# Billing Policy

## Billing Cycle

Customers are billed according to the subscription plan they selected.

Monthly subscriptions are charged once every month on the original subscription date.

Annual subscriptions are charged once every 12 months on the original subscription date.

Customers can view their billing date and payment history from their account billing page.

## Accepted Payment Methods

The company currently accepts:

* Credit cards
* Debit cards
* Supported digital payment methods

Payment methods must have sufficient funds and must not be expired or blocked.

## Failed Payments

If a payment fails, the system will automatically attempt to process the payment again.

Customers may receive an email notification when a payment fails.

If the payment continues to fail, the subscription may be temporarily restricted until a successful payment is received.

Customers should check that their payment method is valid and has sufficient funds.

If the payment method is valid but the payment continues to fail, the customer should contact support.

## Duplicate Charges

If a customer believes they were charged more than once for the same billing period, they should contact customer support.

The support team will review the customer's billing history and determine whether a duplicate charge occurred.

If a duplicate charge is confirmed, the company will process the appropriate correction or refund.

## Changing Payment Information

Customers can update their payment information from their account billing settings.

A new payment method will normally be used for future billing cycles.

Updating payment information does not automatically change the customer's subscription plan.

## Invoices and Receipts

Customers receive a billing receipt after a successful payment.

Previous invoices and receipts can be accessed from the account billing page.

If a customer cannot find an invoice, they should contact support and provide the approximate billing date.

## Taxes

Applicable taxes may be added to the subscription price depending on the customer's location and applicable regulations.

The final amount charged to the customer may therefore be higher than the advertised subscription price.

## Billing Questions

For billing questions that cannot be resolved through the account billing page, customers should contact the support team.

Support may request the customer's account email, transaction date, and payment reference to investigate a billing issue.
